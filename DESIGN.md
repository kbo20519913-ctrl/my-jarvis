# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World

MY JARVIS product UI on Toss TDS Mobile tokens. Light canvas, one functional blue, Pretendard standing in for Toss Product Sans (not redistributed). Not the previous KB yellow cockpit. Not toss.im marketing geometry on product chrome.

## Surfaces

Login and onboarding are full-canvas product flows. Authenticated shell is a white left rail, grey content well, white work surfaces.

## Color

- Canvas `#ffffff`, surface `#f2f4f6`, text `#191f28`, body `#4e5968`, muted `#8b95a1`, border `#e5e8eb`
- Primary `#3182f6`, hover `#2272eb`, on-primary `#ffffff`
- Weak CTA `#e8f3ff` / `#1b64da`
- Danger `#e42939`

## Type

Pretendard Variable (Toss Product Sans named first, unused unless locally installed). Page title 30/600/45. Login display 36/700/54. Body 16/400/24. Primary button 17/600.

## Controls

TDS-shaped fill button: 56px / 16px radius (login, onboarding), 48px / 14px compact. Box field 48px / 12px radius, focus ring on primary. Nav selected is fill primary, 16px radius. Badges are status, not buttons.

## Depth

No shadow tokens. Hierarchy is canvas / surface / white card and 1px `#e5e8eb`.
